I’m Mietakor N. Suah, a junior student at STARZ University and a graphic designer based in Monrovia, Liberia. I create brand identities, social media flyers, and apparel graphics for people and businesses who want their ideas to stand out.

I start by listening to your goals and audience, then shape the design around your message. My aim is to make every project thoughtful, clear, and ready to use.
